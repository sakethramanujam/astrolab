# v1.1 issue: odd/even and secondary tests reject deep real transits

Status: open. **v1.0.0 vetting is unchanged.** These tests stay as they are for every frozen v1.0.0 product.
This note records what we found and the proposed fix.

## Finding (injection–recovery, sector 70, 1,996 injections from run 0)

- `odd_even` rejects more matched injections as the expected SNR rises. The rate is 3–10% below SNR 100
  and 25% above SNR 100. `secondary`, `too_deep` and `variable` show the same pattern: rejection grows
  with depth.
- The test is **significance only**. `exosearch/vet.py` flags `odd_even` when
  `|d_odd - d_even| / hypot(e_odd, e_even) > 3`. There is no minimum fractional difference.
  `secondary` is also `d_sec / e_sec > 3` with no relative-depth condition.
- The errors are `astropy` `compute_stats` errors built from the per-cadence PDCSAP uncertainties of the
  binned light curve. They are white-noise errors, while detection now uses the red-noise-aware CDPP_D.
  When beta_D > 1 the odd/even significance is overstated, and the effect grows with SNR.
- We re-ran 30 matched injections with expected SNR > 50 (20 that failed `odd_even`, 10 that passed) and
  recorded `depth_odd` and `depth_even`. The failing cases have odd/even differences of 3.0–16σ but only
  **0.4–8.5% of the depth (median 2.6%)**. A 1–3% odd/even mismatch is far below any eclipsing-binary
  signature. It is produced by per-epoch sampling (10-min bins vs ingress/egress), local detrending of
  deep transits, and white-noise errors. None of the 30 had partial transits near gaps.
- **The injected ephemeris does not drift.** Injections use a fixed P and T0 (batman, e = 0, BTJD
  throughout), so nothing drifts inside the injection. The *recovered* ephemeris drifts by a median of
  0.06 transit durations over the sector (|P_rec − P_inj| × N_epochs / T14). The `odd_even` failure rate
  barely depends on that drift: 15% at < 0.1 durations and 23% at 0.1–0.25. Drift is not the cause.

## Proposed v1.1 change (agreed with the Scientist)

1. **Require both significance and size.** Flag `odd_even` only if the difference is > 3σ **and**
   `|d_odd − d_even| / mean(d_odd, d_even) > 10%`. Flag `secondary` only if it is > 3σ **and**
   `d_sec / d_primary > 10%`, or the secondary is significant at an absolute depth that cannot come from
   planetary reflection or thermal emission. A purely sigma-based test becomes oversensitive at high SNR.
   With the 10% condition, none of the 20 failing high-SNR injections above would fail.
2. Compute the σ in both tests from the scatter of per-epoch depths (or CDPP_D at the trial duration), not
   from per-cadence pipeline errors.
3. Re-measure the vetting effectiveness, i.e. the per-check true/false rejection rates on labelled
   planets and EBs (tuning stars only), before freezing v1.1. Plan to add an EB/blend injection set so
   that the loss in EB rejection is measured, not assumed.
4. `too_deep` (depth > 5%) and `variable` (phase-0.5 brightening > 5σ) cause the same high-SNR loss for
   giant planets and grazing geometries. Review them at the same time, with the same "significance and
   size" logic for `variable`.

## Also for v1.1: normalisation of S (white-noise calibration)

`python -m exosearch.whitenoise` ran 300 stars of pure Gaussian noise with the real S70 cadence and gaps
through the full chain. Results:

- **The overlapping-window MAD estimator for CDPP_D is unbiased.** Its ratio to the actual scatter of the
  D-window means is 0.996–1.006 for every D.
- The frozen 0.75-d biweight lowers that scatter below the white-noise truth by 2% at 1 h, rising to 17%
  at 6 h. That is real noise suppression, not an estimator bias.
- Per-trial S on white noise is over-dispersed. Its standard deviation is 1.01 at D = 1 h, rising to
  1.22 at 6 h.
  - Part comes from the box contrast keeping variance that the local running-mean CDPP_D no longer has
    after detrending.
  - The other part comes from the final S (coverage-weighted N_tr) leaving out the out-of-transit
    baseline error. At duty cycles D/P up to 0.5 this matters: S/S_scan is a median 1.16 for peaks
    with N_tr > 10.
- Max S on white noise has a median of 5.48 (S_scan 5.26, p95 5.95). The Gaussian extreme-value median
  for the analytic number of independent (P, T0, D) boxes (3.4 × 10^5) is 4.61. The per-D
  over-dispersion explains most of the difference.
- Fix: include the out-of-transit term in S. Then either normalise S per duration with the white-noise
  calibration (z-score per D), or measure CDPP_D from the scatter of the box contrast itself. Re-derive S*.

### Effective number of trials (white noise, out-of-transit term restored)

`python -m exosearch.whitenoise_neff` restores the out-of-transit term at each stored white-noise peak,
S_oot = S · sqrt(1 − q) with q = D/P (σ_δ² = CDPP_D²/N_tr · 1/(1 − q)). The peak is selected by the scan
statistic, which already carries that term, so restoring it changes the value at the chosen peak, not the peak;
re-running the scan would reproduce the stored peaks.

| statistic | median max | Gumbel μ, β | expected max | N_eff (unit sd) | joint fit: trial sd, N_eff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S_scan | 5.26 | 5.15, 0.290 | 5.32 | 7.8 × 10^6 (from μ), 5.0 × 10^6 (from β) | 1.26, 4.9 × 10^4 |
| S (v1.0.0) | 5.48 | 5.40, 0.567 | 5.72 | not Gaussian-max-like (KS p 0.014; joint fit does not converge) | — |
| **S_oot** | 5.21 | 5.10, 0.292 | **5.27** | 6.0 × 10^6 (μ), 3.9 × 10^6 (β) | **1.25, 4.3 × 10^4** (KS p 0.86) |

A unit-variance reading needs N_eff ≈ 4–6 × 10^6 per star, more than the 2.1 × 10^5 (P, D) grid points, so the
consistent reading is the joint one: per-trial sd ≈ 1.25 (matching the measured 1.01–1.22 per D) and
**N_eff ≈ 4.3 × 10^4 independent trials per star; expected white-noise max 5.27.** At α = 1/N_stars (10,244) this gives
a white-noise S* of 7.35; the vetted inverted-null S* (12.4) is far above it, so the tail is set by non-white
residuals, not by the trial count.

### For v1.1: re-derive S* jointly after the normalisation fix

After S includes the out-of-transit term (and the per-D z-score or box-contrast CDPP), re-derive S* in one step from
the vetted inverted null with the same quality cut, the same GPD u rule (lowest u ≥ 6 with k ≥ 50 and ξ stable within
1 bootstrap SE), and the same N_eff from the white-noise calibration (re-measured with the fixed statistic) as the
white-noise floor. Do not carry the v1.0.0 S* across a change in the statistic: completeness, FAP and the survivors
list are re-scored together.

### For v1.1: momentum-dump proximity veto

Null peaks with ≤ 2 in-transit epochs fall within 0.1 d of a reaction-wheel momentum dump in 8.8% of epochs against
1.3% for random epochs on the same stars (×6.8; gap/orbit edges ×1.5; multi-epoch peaks ×1.3; vetted tail: no excess).
Proposal: flag (reject when ≤ 2 epochs, warn otherwise) any transit epoch whose centre is within 0.1 d of a momentum
dump (quality bit 32/1024 or the sector's dump times), and recount N_tr without those epochs before S is computed.
Evaluate on injections first (cost in completeness) and register the window before applying it to the null.

### For v1.1: red-noise floor, beta_D >= 1 (Pont et al. 2006 convention)

The red-noise correction should never shrink the error bar: use CDPP_D' = max(CDPP_D, sigma_w / sqrt(N_D)), i.e.
beta_D' = max(1, beta_D). `python -m exosearch.beta_floor` applies it post hoc to the stored S70 flipped-null peaks
(S' = S · min(1, beta_D) at the stored peak; the peak is not re-chosen, so S' is a lower bound on the floored max).
Tuning stars passing the quality cut, n = 5,881 (snapshot):

- beta_D < 1 at the peak: 2,932 stars (50%); < 0.8: 230 (3.9%); < 0.5: 7. The distribution is centred on 1
  (median 1.000), so most of the sub-1 values are estimator scatter, but the floor still removes a systematic gain.
- Vetted tail above S = 6: 116 of 288 peaks (40%) have beta_D < 1. Above the interim S* (12.39): none; no vetted null
  peak reaches S* yet (largest 11.3).
- S* on the same sample: 12.07 (95% 10.13–14.68; u = 6.25, k = 179) without the floor, **11.62** (95% 9.72–14.12;
  u = 6.25, k = 151) with it: **−0.45**, inside the statistical CI and about twice the u-systematic (±0.25).
- Case with beta_D < 1 in the real search: TIC 71334169 (S105, S 21.5, beta_D 0.32 at D = 0.13 d). The per-cadence
  scatter (sigma_w 3,300 ppm, against a 490 ppm pipeline error) is dominated by a coherent pulsation at 22.97 c/d
  (P = 62.7 min, semi-amplitude ~3,300 ppm; further modes at 21.96 and 21.08 c/d, δ Scuti-like). The residuals have
  std/MAD = 0.90 and no point beyond 5 MAD: no outliers or flares, and the MAD did not collapse. A ~1 h oscillation
  averages out inside 1–6 h windows, so CDPP_D falls far below sigma_w / sqrt(N_D) and the sigma_w-based beta_D is < 1.
  The floor gives S' = 6.8 for this peak. The v1.1 fix also needs high-frequency prewhitening (or a sigma_w measured
  after removing coherent oscillations) for pulsators.

### For v1.1: S from the exact box depth (depth audit, 2026-11-10)

v1.0.0 computes S from `depth_box`, the depth astropy's BLS `fast` method reports at the scan peak. At large D/P
that value can exceed the depth of any box at the reported (P, D, T0): TIC 71334169 (S105) has `depth_box` 463 ppm
against 33 ppm from `compute_stats`, an unweighted box mean and a weighted box mean (85 ppm from a `slow` refit).
Every other depth consumer (Rp, `too_deep`, `no_dip`, odd/even, secondary, `rp_too_large`, the deep checks, the
site) already uses `depth` from `compute_stats`; injection matching uses P and T0 only. `depth_box/depth`: median
1.01 on the S70 flipped null (0.9% of rows > 2× or `depth` ≤ 0; 3.6% at D/P 0.2–0.6, ~0% at D/P < 0.05), 1.09 on
the S105 real search, 1.000 on matched injections (none > 2×). The inflation lands on non-transit peaks, so it raises
the null tail and S* but not the injections. Interim layer: `S_exact = depth·sqrt(n_tr)/cdpp_d` on every population
(`exosearch/interim.py: exact_s`). S70 S* on the same sample moves 12.06 → 11.20; completeness at expected S 15–25
rises from 64% to 78% detected (55% → 68% vetted). v1.1: compute S in the search from the exact box depth at each
(P, D, T0) candidate (or refine the top peaks with the exact depth before taking the max).

### For v1.1: pulsator prewhitening

Coherent oscillations faster than the trial durations (δ Scuti, γ Dor high-order modes, sdB p-modes) inflate
sigma_w but average out of CDPP_D, so beta_D < 1 and S is inflated (TIC 71334169: 22.97 c/d, ~3,300 ppm). v1.1:
before detrending, iteratively remove sinusoids above ~5 c/d (Lomb–Scargle peak, fit and subtract amplitude, phase
and frequency; stop when the peak falls below S/N 4 in amplitude, or after 20 terms), log the removed frequencies
and amplitudes per star, and measure sigma_w after prewhitening. Validate on injections: the transit (≥ 0.04 d,
power mostly below 5 c/d) must not be absorbed; cap removed power and refuse frequencies within 10% of 1/D.

### For v1.1: beta treatment (comparison on the S70 flipped null, interim layer, S_exact base)

`python -m exosearch.stat_options`. beta' replaces beta_D at the stored peak (S' = S_exact·beta/beta'), the same rule
on the null and on the injections; S* are lower bounds (peaks not re-chosen). sigma_beta = 1/sqrt(2 N_w),
N_w = n_cad·2 min / D (median sigma_beta 0.047, so option c floors at ~0.91). Prior for d: per-D median and
between-star variance of the star-quality beta_D of all kept S70 stars (medians 0.93–1.02).

| option | S* (95% CI) | null peaks moved > 1% vs a | injections moved > 1% vs a | completeness 15–25 det / vetted | 10–15 det / vetted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1.0 S (depth_box) | 12.06 (10.15–14.86) | — | — | 64% / 55% | 3% / 2% |
| a: S_exact, no floor | 11.20 (9.38–13.46) | 0 | 0 | 78% / 68% | 7% / 6% |
| b: beta ≥ 1 | 10.66 (9.29–12.87) | 2,890 down | 656 | 82% / 71% | 11% / 10% |
| c: beta ≥ 1 − 2σ_beta | 10.75 (9.40–12.91) | 603 down | 122 | 82% / 70% | 10% / 9% |
| d: empirical Bayes | 11.05 (9.39–13.01) | 55 down, 507 up | 192 | 79% / 68% | 9% / 8% |

(6,424 tuning + quality null stars; 2,342 S70 injections in the allowed range; ≥ 25 bins unchanged across options.)
Recommendation for v1.1: option c inside the statistic (only significant sub-unity beta is floored, so white-noise
estimator scatter is not systematically penalised), with prewhitening first so pulsators no longer reach it.
