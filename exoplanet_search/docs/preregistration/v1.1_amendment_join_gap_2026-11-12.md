## Why
In the top 1% of vetted scrambled rows (option b, the population the S* fit uses), 13.9% of in-transit points lay

Leakage of real signals was small (0/80 vetted; 4/80 in the rule-set top 1%) and dropping it did not move S*.

## The fix (scrambled null only; the flipped null code is unchanged)
In `exosearch.v11.scramble.segment_rotate`, after each segment is rotated, a time offset is inserted at its wrap
point (the cadence where the segment's original first sample now sits): that cadence and every later cadence move
later by `JOIN_GAP_D = BREAK_TOLERANCE_D + cadence`, cumulatively over wraps. No mask and no taper; flux and flux
errors are untouched by the fix.
* `BREAK_TOLERANCE_D = 0.5 d`: `lightcurve.detrend` calls wotan `flatten(..., break_tolerance=0.5)`, and wotan splits
  where `diff(t) > break_tolerance`.
* cadence = median spacing of the gap-edge-masked input; sector 70 2-min data: 120 s = 0.0013888... d.
* **Offset = 0.5 + 0.0013889 = 0.5013889 d** (recorded per row as `scr_join_gap_d`, with `scr_cadence_d`,
  `scr_wrap_t0`/`scr_wrap_t1` = last cadence before and first cadence after each inserted gap).
* Unit tests (`tests/test_v11_scramble.py`): the offset equals the value parsed from `lightcurve.detrend` plus the
  cadence; a rotated linear ramp gives a detrender break at the wrap (wotan `get_gaps_indexes`) and no box dip there
  (< 3 sigma and no larger than at a real segment start, versus > 10 sigma without the gap).
* Known side effects: the scrambled light curve's baseline grows by one offset per wrap, so its period grid extends
  (pmax = baseline/2); momentum-dump times are not shifted; the inserted gaps are not edge-trimmed (no mask, by
  ruling). The shifted time stamps also break a real signal's phase coherence across a wrap (planted check, 10 seeds:
  max sigma P=1/3/6 d from 7.70/7.26/7.82 to 5.23/3.34/2.88).

## Pass test (the fix itself)
Population: top 1% (ceil(0.01 n)) of vetted scrambled rows by S_opt_b on the paired set after the leakage drop.
Statistic: pooled over those rows, k = in-transit cadences (|phase| < duration/2 at the row's option-b peak, on the
detrended scrambled light curve) lying within one trial duration of a former join (the cadence before or after an
inserted gap), out of n in-transit cadences. Baseline p0 = the fraction of each light curve's cadences within one
duration of a former join, weighted by that row's in-transit count. **Pass if k <= the one-sided 99% binomial
bound, binom.ppf(0.99, n, p0).** Otherwise the fix failed and we stop (no third registration is pushed as a pass
candidate). The per-row mean fraction and the any-hit/random-epoch numbers are reported alongside.
