**Post hoc.** Follows the join-gap amendment (`v1.1_amendment_join_gap_2026-11-12.md`, 8025edc), whose pre-registered
pass test failed on the rerun (top 1% of vetted scrambled rows: 1,266 of 21,627 in-transit cadences near a former join,

**Approved by Saketh 2026-11-12 8:47 AM PT.** Committed and pushed before any rerun output exists.

## The change (scrambled null only; the flipped null, the detrender, the beta rule, S* and vetting are unchanged)
Rule (8) in `exosearch.v11.scramble`: each inserted wrap gap gets exactly the edge trim real gaps get in rule (1),
through the same function, `lightcurve.gap_edge_mask`, with the same configuration values (`scramble.gap_d` = 0.5 d,
`scramble.gap_trim_d` = 0.3 d): cadences within 0.3 d after the first cadence after the inserted gap and within 0.3 d
before the last cadence before it are dropped. This copies the existing real-gap rule; it is not a new or tuned
parameter. Real gaps are not trimmed a second time. The join gap itself (0.5 d + one cadence, 0.5013889 d) is unchanged.
* Test `test_inserted_gaps_trimmed_exactly_like_real_gaps`: around inserted gaps, the dropped cadences equal those
  `gap_edge_mask` drops around a real gap of the same geometry, and nothing else is dropped.

## Pass test (unchanged from 8025edc)
Top 1% (ceil(0.01 n)) of vetted scrambled rows by S_opt_b on the paired set after the leakage drop. Pooled over those
rows: k = in-transit cadences (|phase| < duration/2 at the row's option-b peak, on the detrended scrambled light curve)
within one trial duration of a former join (the cadence before or after an inserted gap, before trimming), out of n
in-transit cadences. Baseline p0 = each light curve's fraction of cadences within one duration of a former join,
weighted by that row's in-transit count. **Pass if k <= binom.ppf(0.99, n, p0).** The rule-set population (top 1% of
all scrambled rows) is reported alongside, as are the per-row mean and the any-hit/random-epoch numbers.

## If it fails
We stop patching the joins. The choice between the nulls (which one sets S*, or a dated rule for combining them) is
then decided on the evidence already gathered, in a further dated amendment signed off by Saketh.

## If it passes
Rerun the beta test (exosearch.v11.beta_rows; beta_select's rule functions unchanged) on the new pairs as the third
registration; the 7326715e and fa3bbf8 failures stay on record. If the gate passes, proceed as registered; if it
fails with the join excess gone, S* = the larger of the two option-b thresholds, pending Saketh's sign-off.
