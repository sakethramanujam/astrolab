## 2. Post hoc fixes (found after results were seen)

Both fixes below were found after we had seen results. They are post hoc and are labelled that way here and in
any paper.

### 2a. Scrambled null (post hoc)

* Why: the v1.0 scramble permutes ~1-day blocks of the quality-masked light curve before the gap-edge trim and
  across orbit gaps. That moves gap-edge ramps and scattered light into clean stretches, and blocks that span a
  gap create step edges. Sector 106 has two gaps (about 5.8 d of gap time, against about 2.5 d in sector 70)
  and its scrambled S* came out near 23 against about 12 to 14 for the flipped null. A handful of extreme stars
  drive that, e.g. TIC 144575712 at S = 69 (diagnosis in the 2026-11-11 null write-up). This was the main
  cause of the beta gate failure.
* Registered rule (v1.1 scramble):
  1. Start from the quality-masked light curve and apply `lightcurve.gap_edge_mask` (gap 0.5 d, same trim as
     the detrender) before scrambling. Masked cadences are dropped.
  2. Split into contiguous segments at gaps longer than 0.5 d.
  3. Rotate each segment's flux and flux error together by its own independent random cyclic offset. Time
     stamps stay fixed. No sample crosses a gap. Offsets are drawn from a seeded generator (task seed as in
     v1.0, `nulltest.seed_for`) and recorded per star in the output row.
  4. Fallback, only for a segment too short for a meaningful rotation (shorter than 3x the longest trial
     duration): shuffle within that segment in contiguous blocks of at least 3x the longest trial duration.
     A segment shorter than one such block is left unrotated, and the row records that.
  5. Parameters live in `configs/v1.1.json` (`scramble` section), so they enter the v1.1 config hash.
* Implementation: branch `agy/t1-scramble-segment-rotation` (docs/v1.1_scramble_amendment.md), merged only
  after review.

### 2b. Prewhitening (post hoc)

* Why: the first v1.1 prewhitening fix (commit `ca9d305`) was rejected on review. Its comb guard had no upper
  bound, so it protected real delta Scuti pulsations (TIC 346626001 was tagged as a comb at m = 26 to 36), and one
  false comb could block about 74% of the spectrum. Validation could not detect a filter that removes nothing.
* Validation requirement for the second pass:
  * Use NEW injection seeds only. The seeds that exposed the failure (the v1.0 `injection_v1` run-0 seeds of the
    sector-70 hosts and the `validate.py` base seed 20261110) are excluded. The new set uses base seed 20261112
    and injection runs 12 to 15 (v1.0 used runs 0 to 11). Host selection is redrawn with the new base seed.
  * Real delta Scuti stars are a pass/fail check: a list of known delta Scuti / high-frequency pulsators in the
    sector-70 target list is fixed in this file's companion list before the run. The filter fails if it leaves
    the dominant pulsation modes in place (the removal criterion is fixed with the list, before the run).
  * Transit recovery on the new seeds must not drop relative to prewhitening off, within the binomial error.

## 3. Known-planet recovery test (new, pass/fail)

Every confirmed planet or active TOI (TFOPWG disposition not FP/FA) on a searched star with
expected S >= S* and at least 2 transits in the sector data must be recovered.

* Expected S = depth_exp x sqrt(n_tr) / CDPP_D, with n_tr the transits of the catalogue ephemeris that survive the
  v1.1 masks and CDPP_D our noise at the catalogue duration. depth_exp is the catalogue depth scaled by the
  contamination ratio of the aperture we search (target flux fraction of our light curve relative to the
  catalogue's). The ratio is recorded per star.
* Recovered means the reported peak period is within the period-grid plateau half-width of the catalogue period
  (`products/interim/p_grid_halfwidth_v1.csv`), or is a 2x or 1/2x alias and is flagged as one.
* Any miss is listed with its cause. A miss that is not explained by a recorded, pre-declared mechanism fails
  the test and blocks production.
* The clean v1.0 audit (`docs/v1.1_checks/known_planet_audit_clean_2026-11-11/`) is the before table.
