# v1.1 Prewhitening Amendment: Task T0 Policy (2026-11-11)

## Status and Context

This amendment records the design and implementation for Task T0 (blocker) described in
`exoplanet_search/docs/v1.1_remaining.md` and addresses the prewhitening validation failure documented
in `exoplanet_search/docs/v1.1_validation.md` at commit `130df5dc`.

At commit `130df5dc`, prewhitening above 5 c/d failed validation criteria P1 (recovery loss > 2 pp)
and P2 (depth suppression > 2%):
- T14 0–1 h: Δrec −0.117 [−0.191, −0.064], depth ratio 0.53 / 0.66 (P1 failure)
- T14 1–2 h: Δrec −0.048 [−0.073, −0.025], depth ratio 0.71 / 0.89 (P1 and P2 failure)
- P 0.5–1 d: Δrec −0.099 [−0.136, −0.068], depth ratio 0.67 / 0.90 (P1 and P2 failure)
- USP T14 0–1 h: Δrec −0.078 [−0.157, −0.020], depth ratio 0.56 / 0.66 (P1 failure)
- USP T14 1–2 h: Δrec −0.092 [−0.139, −0.052], depth ratio 0.62 / 0.92 (P1 and P2 failure)
- USP T14 2–3 h: depth ratio 0.71 / 0.93 (P2 failure)

Prewhitening removed terms on 995 of 3,442 main-set stars (28.9%) and 340 of 813 USP stars (41.8%).
These rates were measured on injected stars (where transit signals are present), indicating that transit
harmonics above 5 c/d were being fitted and subtracted as sinusoids.

---

## Root Cause Analysis

1. **Comb Guard Limitations at Commit 130df5dc:**
   The original comb guard only checked multiples $m \in \{1, 2, 3\}$. For $f \ge 5$ c/d, this only
   protected orbital periods $P = m / f \le 3 / 5 = 0.6$ days. Transits with $P \in [0.5, 5.0]$ days have
   harmonics at $f \ge 5$ c/d corresponding to $m \ge 4$ (up to $m = 30+$), which were completely unprotected.
   Furthermore, requiring both $2f$ and $3f$ to independently achieve $\text{SNR} \ge 4.0$ failed whenever
   harmonics fell near nodal zeros of the transit box sinc transfer function.

2. **Lessons from Early Revisions (ca9d305):**
   An initial attempt to expand $m \ge 2$ checked single amplitude ratios against a theoretical box model
   without upper bounds on duration poles, used non-local noise estimations, and excluded full harmonic series
   across the spectrum. This led to over-protection: rotation, red noise, and $\gamma$ Dor modes caused false
   comb matches on genuine $\delta$ Scuti pulsators, shutting off prewhitening and failing to clean real modes.

## Adopted Policy and Implementation

All thresholds have been moved into `configs/v1.1.json["prewhiten"]` so that any policy changes are fully
tracked by `settings.config_hash()`. The parameters were fixed a priori and not tuned on the acceptance
injection set.

### 1. Star-Level Gate
Before iterative prewhitening, the peak Lomb-Scargle SNR on $[f_{\text{min}}, f_{\text{Nyq}}]$ must exceed
`snr_detect` (default 5.0, set in `configs/v1.1.json`). Stars with quiet spectra skip prewhitening without
removing terms.

### 2. Multi-Harmonic Planetary Comb Guard with Phase Consistency
To protect planetary transits while preserving genuine stellar pulsations:
- **Orbital Harmonic Scan:** Candidate peaks $f$ are evaluated across candidate fundamentals $f_0 = f / m$
  with $P = m / f \in [0.2, 5.0]$ days (`comb_p_range_d`), without arbitrary truncation.
- **Local Noise Floor:** Amplitude at candidate fundamental $f_0$ is evaluated against the local noise floor
  measured around $f_0$, requiring $\text{SNR} \ge \text{comb\_min\_snr}$ (default 2.0).
- **Two-Sided Box Ladder Model:** Transit duration grid $[0.0208, 0.25]$ days (`comb_durations_d`, starting at
  0.5 h to protect sub-hour transits) is bounded by $m \cdot q < 0.8$, eliminating model poles and keeping the
  amplitude ratio bounded.
- **Phase Consistency:** A periodic transit box has consistent Fourier phases pointing to a single epoch $T_0$.
  Candidate peaks must satisfy phase consistency with $f_0$ within `comb_phase_tol` (0.25 cycle fraction).
- **Secondary Harmonic Verification:** A secondary harmonic $k \cdot f_0$ ($k=2$, or $k=3$ if $m=2$) must also
  show $\text{SNR} \ge \text{comb\_min\_snr}$, phase consistency with $T_0$, and consistent simultaneous box
  ladder amplitude ratio within `comb_ratio_tol` (2.0).
- **Fallback Multiples 1..3:** Multiples $m \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ check $2f$ and $3f$ requiring $\text{SNR} \ge \text{comb\_min\_snr}$,
  `comb_ratio_tol`, and phase alignment.
- **Bounded Exclusion:** When a comb is confirmed, harmonic exclusion windows ($\pm 1/\text{baseline}$) are
  applied only up to $h_{\text{max}} = 1.5 / (D_{\text{min}} f_0)$ and capped at `comb_max_coverage` (20%),
  preventing comb masks from blanking the residual spectrum.

### 3. Two-Halves Sector Coherence Check
For candidate pulsations, stationary physical oscillation is verified across both observation halves:
- Both halves must achieve amplitude $\ge \text{half\_min\_snr} \times \text{noise}_{\text{half}}$ (default 2.0).
- Amplitude ratio between halves must satisfy $A_1 / A_2 \le \text{half\_amp\_ratio}$ (default 5.0), accommodating beating pairs.
- Phase coherence relative to the full fit must satisfy $\cos(\Delta \phi) \ge \text{half\_cos\_phase}$ (default -0.5).
Non-coherent single-sector transients or glitches are rejected without sinusoid subtraction.

### 4. Validation Gate: Pulsator-Efficacy Arm (P4)
In `exoplanet_search/exosearch/v11/validate.py`, criterion P4 (`pulsator_efficacy`) has been added to `all_pass`:
it verifies on seeded synthetic stars with realistic white noise, red noise, and rotation that genuine $\delta$ Scuti
modes are removed (median removal $\ge 80\%$, star-level minimum $\ge 50\%$). A policy that removes nothing cannot pass.
