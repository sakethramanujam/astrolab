# v1.1 Prewhitening Amendment: Task T0 Policy (2026-11-11)

## Status and Context

This amendment records the design and implementation for Task T0 (blocker) described in
`exoplanet_search/docs/v1.1_remaining.md` and addresses the prewhitening validation failure documented
in `exoplanet_search/docs/v1.1_validation.md` at commit `130df5dc`.

At commit `130df5dc`, prewhitening above 5 c/d failed validation criteria P1 (recovery loss > 2 pp)
and P2 (depth suppression > 2%):
- T14 0–1 h: Δrec −0.117 [−0.191, −0.064], depth ratio 0.53 / 0.66 (P1 failure)
- T14 1–2 h: Δrec −0.048 [−0.073, −0.025], depth ratio 0.71 / 0.89 (P1 and P2 failure)
- P 0.5–1 d: Δrec −0.099 [−0.136, −0.068], depth ratio 0.67 / 0.90 (P1 and P2 failure)
- USP T14 0–1 h: Δrec −0.078 [−0.157, −0.020], depth ratio 0.56 / 0.66 (P1 failure)
- USP T14 1–2 h: Δrec −0.092 [−0.139, −0.052], depth ratio 0.62 / 0.92 (P1 and P2 failure)
- USP T14 2–3 h: depth ratio 0.71 / 0.93 (P2 failure)

Prewhitening removed terms on 995 of 3,442 main-set stars (28.9%) and 340 of 813 USP stars (41.8%),
indicating that the previous policy was picking up pure noise on quiet stars as well as carving out
Fourier power from transit signals.

---

## Root Cause Analysis

1. **Trials factor and null noise threshold:**
   Evaluating Lomb-Scargle power across ~40,000 independent frequency bins ($f_{\text{min}} = 5$ c/d to
   $f_{\text{Nyq}} \approx 360$ c/d with $5\times$ oversampling on a 27-day baseline) produces a maximum noise
   peak SNR of $\sim 4.2–4.5$ on pure Gaussian white noise. The previous stopping threshold $\text{SNR} < 4.0$
   inevitably triggered false removals on 15–20% of quiet stars from pure white noise alone.

2. **Harmonic coverage of the previous comb guard:**
   The original comb guard checked only multiples $m \in \{1, 2, 3\}$. For $f \ge 5$ c/d, this only
   covered orbital periods $P = m / f \le 3 / 5 = 0.6$ days. Planets with $P \in [0.5, 5.0]$ days have
   harmonics above 5 c/d corresponding to $m \ge 4$ (up to $m = 30+$), which were completely unprotected.
   Furthermore, requiring both $2f$ and $3f$ to independently achieve $\text{SNR} \ge 4.0$ failed whenever
   either harmonic fell near a nodal zero of the transit box $\text{sinc}(j q)$ transfer function.

3. **Lack of temporal stationarity check:**
   Real stellar pulsations (such as $\delta$ Scuti p-modes) are continuous, stationary physical oscillations
   sustained across the entire observation. Transient noise bursts or non-periodic systematic variations
   were previously vulnerable to being fitted as coherent sinusoids.

## Adopted Policy and Implementation

The prewhitening implementation in `exoplanet_search/exosearch/v11/prewhiten.py` has been updated with three
complementary guards:

### 1. Star-Level Null Calibration
Before any sinusoids are removed, the star's maximum Lomb-Scargle SNR in the search band $[f_{\text{min}}, f_{\text{Nyq}}]$
is compared against an initial detection threshold calibrated on null (flipped) light curves:
$$\text{SNR}_{\text{detect}} = \max(\text{cfg.get}(\text{"snr\_detect"}, 6.0), \text{cfg}[\text{"snr\_stop"}])$$
Because maximum noise peaks on null light curves never exceed $\sim 4.5$, non-pulsating stars immediately
return with 0 terms removed. Real $\delta$ Scuti pulsators have SNRs of 20 to 500+ and easily pass.

### 2. Comprehensive Transit Comb Guard
For candidate peaks $f$, the comb guard searches across candidate multiples $m \ge 1$ covering planetary periods
$P = m / f \in [0.2, 5.0]$ days:
- For $m \in \{1, 2, 3\}$: checks $2f$ and $3f$ against the box harmonic ladder (`box_ladder`).
- For $m \ge 2$: fits the fundamental orbital frequency $f_0 = f / m$ and tests whether the amplitude ratio
  $A(f_0) / A(f)$ matches the box ladder expectation $R_{\text{theo}}(m, q) = m \frac{|\sin(\pi q)|}{|\sin(\pi m q)|}$
  for a physical transit duration $D \in [1, 6]$ hours ($q = D / P$).
When a match is found:
- The peak is logged in `combs` (`prewhiten_comb` count incremented).
- The entire harmonic series $k \times (f / m)$ is excluded from subsequent picks ($\pm 2/\text{baseline}$).
- The peak is not removed.

### 3. Two-Halves Sector Coherence Check
For candidates that are not transit combs, prewhitening verifies that the oscillation is stationary across both
halves of the observation baseline ($t < t_{\text{mid}}$ and $t \ge t_{\text{mid}}$):
- Both halves must show significant amplitude above the local noise floor ($A_{\text{half}} \ge 2.5 \times \text{noise}_{\text{half}}$).
- Fitted amplitudes must be consistent across halves ($A_1 / A_2 \in [1/3, 3]$).
- The phase in both halves must be coherent with the global fit ($\cos(\phi_{\text{half}} - \phi_{\text{full}}) \ge 0.3$).
Non-coherent peaks are excluded from subsequent picks without removing flux.

## Verification and Accept Criteria

### Unit Tests
New unit tests in `exoplanet_search/tests/test_v11_prewhiten.py` and existing tests in `exoplanet_search/tests/test_v11.py`
confirm:
1. `test_null_light_curve_not_prewhitened`: 0 terms removed on pure noise light curves.
2. `test_usp_depth_preserved_by_comb_guard`: USP transit depths ($P = 0.7$ d, $T_{14} = 1.2$ h) preserved within 2%, 0 terms removed.
3. `test_transit_with_pulsation_removes_pulsation_only`: cleans genuine 22.97 c/d pulsation while protecting transit depth.
4. `test_two_halves_coherence_rejects_transient_glitch`: non-stationary bursts are rejected.
5. `test_transit_comb_matches_harmonics`: verifies comb detection across periods and harmonic indices.
