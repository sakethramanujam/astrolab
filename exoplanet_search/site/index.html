Searching starlight for passing planets

We download NASA TESS light curves sector by sector, look for the tiny periodic dips a planet makes when it crosses its star, throw out the usual impostors, and check everything against known planets. This page updates after every run.

⚠️
Nothing here is a discovery. Every row below is a threshold-crossing signal produced by an automated screen. A signal only becomes a planet candidate after human vetting, other sectors, pixel-level checks and follow-up observations, and only becomes a planet after independent confirmation. "Known" means our search re-found something already catalogued.
